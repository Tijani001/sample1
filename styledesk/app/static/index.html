How it works
  1. Train a voice on a few pieces of your own writing (Train voice).
  2. Paste any text here and press Check.
  3. See what doesn't sound like you, and rewrite it in your voice.
Create your first voice. A voice is a writing style learned from your samples, for example “My blog”, “Work emails” or “Our newsroom”. You can have as many as you like.
