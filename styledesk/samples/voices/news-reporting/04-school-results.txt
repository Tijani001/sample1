Schools record best exam results in a decade
Secondary schools in the city have recorded their best exam results in a decade, with 68 per cent of pupils achieving a pass in maths and English.

The figure is up from 61 per cent last year, the education department said.

Education director Michael Osei said the results reflected a programme of extra tutoring introduced after the pandemic. "Teachers and pupils deserve enormous credit," Mr Osei said.

Two schools in the east of the city remain below the national average. The department said it would work closely with both.

Individual school results will be published on the council website next week.
