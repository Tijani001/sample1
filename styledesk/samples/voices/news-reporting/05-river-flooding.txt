Residents evacuated as river bursts its banks
Around 40 households were evacuated overnight after the River Tamsin burst its banks following two days of heavy rain.

Firefighters used boats to reach five families trapped on the upper floors of their homes, the fire service said.

No injuries were reported.

Station commander Peter Ellis said crews had worked through the night. "The water rose very quickly and people did exactly the right thing by calling us," Mr Ellis said.

The Environment Agency said river levels were expected to fall by Friday. Residents were advised to check its website before returning home.
