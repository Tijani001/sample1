Why I stopped following recipes exactly
I used to measure everything. Every gram, every pinch. If a recipe said a quarter teaspoon of salt, I used a quarter teaspoon of salt.

My food was fine. It was also kind of boring.

Then my grandmother came to stay for a week. She doesn't own a measuring cup. She tastes as she goes and adjusts. When I asked how she knew how much garlic to use, she looked at me like I'd asked how she knew to breathe.

I'm not at her level. I don't think I ever will be. But I taste my food now while I'm cooking it, and that one change made a bigger difference than any recipe ever did.
