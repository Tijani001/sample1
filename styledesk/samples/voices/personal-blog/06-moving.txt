Moving cities at 34
Everyone told me moving at 34 would be hard. They were right, just not in the ways I expected.

Packing was easy. Finding an apartment was fine. What's hard is the small stuff. I don't know where to get a good haircut. I don't have a favorite coffee place. Nobody at the corner shop knows my name.

I'm learning that you can't rush that part. You just keep showing up at the same places until they start to feel like yours.

Three months in, the guy at the bakery said "the usual?" this morning. I nearly cried.
