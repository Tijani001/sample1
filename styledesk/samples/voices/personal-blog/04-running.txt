What nobody tells you about your first 10k
I ran my first 10k on Sunday. I didn't train properly, I went out too fast, and I walked twice. I still finished, and I'm weirdly proud of it.

Nobody told me how loud it would be. People cheering, music at every corner, someone dressed as a banana handing out jelly beans. It was a lot.

Nobody told me about the hill at kilometre seven either. I'm still a little mad about that hill.

Will I do another one? Yes. Will I train for it this time? Also yes. Probably.
