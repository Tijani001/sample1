I keep killing my plants and I finally know why
Okay, confession. I've killed eleven plants in two years. Eleven. I counted.

I always blamed the light, or the pots, or bad luck. Turns out the problem was me. I was watering them every single day because I thought that's what caring for them meant.

A woman at the garden center laughed when I told her. Not in a mean way. She just said most plants die from too much love, not too little.

So now I water once a week and leave them alone. Two months in and nothing's died. I'm calling that a win.
