I tried waking up at 5am for a month
I'm not a morning person. Never have been. So when a friend dared me to get up at 5am for thirty days, I said yes before I could think about it.

The first week was rough. I hit snooze more than I'd like to admit. By day ten I'd stopped fighting it, and that's when it got interesting.

Here's what surprised me. I didn't get more done. I just got different things done. The quiet hour before everyone else woke up turned into my writing time, and I wrote more in that month than in the six before it.

Would I keep doing it? Probably not every day. But I get why people swear by it now.
