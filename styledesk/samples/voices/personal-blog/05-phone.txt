I put my phone in a drawer after 8pm
It started as an experiment. My screen time report said I was averaging five hours a day, and I didn't believe it. So I checked. It was right.

Now the phone goes in a kitchen drawer at 8pm. Not on silent. Not face down. In the drawer.

The first few nights I kept reaching for it. My hand just did it on its own. That's a bit scary when you think about it.

After a week I was reading again. After two I was sleeping better. I don't want to oversell it. It's a drawer. But it's the best free fix I've found in years.
