Historic market reopens after £12m restoration
The city's Victorian covered market has reopened to the public after a two-year restoration costing £12m.

More than 60 traders have signed leases, including seven who traded in the market before it closed.

The mayor, Leila Haddad, cut the ribbon at a ceremony on Saturday morning. "This building is part of the heart of the city," Ms Haddad said.

The restoration was funded by the council and a heritage grant. Repairs included a new roof and the renovation of the original ironwork.

The market will open six days a week from 8am.
