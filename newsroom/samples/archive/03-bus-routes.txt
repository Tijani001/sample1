Three bus routes to be cut under transport shake-up
Three bus routes serving the northern suburbs will be withdrawn next year under plans announced by the regional transport authority.

The authority said fewer than 200 passengers a day used each of the routes. It said the savings would be spent on more frequent services on the busiest corridors.

Local MP Grace Thompson said the decision would leave older residents isolated. "For many people these buses are a lifeline," Ms Thompson said.

A public consultation opens on Monday and runs for six weeks.
