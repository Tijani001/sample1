Hospital waiting times fall for first time in two years
Waiting times at the city's main hospital have fallen for the first time in two years, according to figures published on Thursday.

The share of patients seen within four hours rose to 71 per cent in October, up from 64 per cent a year earlier.

Hospital chief executive Dr Ruth Adeyemi said extra evening staff had made the difference. "Our teams have worked incredibly hard," Dr Adeyemi said.

Patient groups welcomed the improvement but said the hospital was still short of its target of 95 per cent.

The hospital said it would publish its winter plan next month.
