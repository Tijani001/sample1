Council approves budget with 4 per cent rise in rents
The city council has approved a budget that raises social housing rents by 4 per cent, despite opposition from tenants' groups.

Councillors voted 31 to 19 in favour of the plan on Tuesday evening after a three-hour debate.

Council leader Amara Okafor said the increase was needed to fund repairs to ageing tower blocks. "We cannot keep patching buildings that need proper investment," Ms Okafor said.

The Tenants' Federation said the rise would push some families into arrears. Its chair, David Mensah, said the organisation would campaign against the change.

The new rents take effect in April.
