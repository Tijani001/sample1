Fair or prejudicial? 🗞

Prejudicial.“Corrupt” presumes guilt.

Prejudicial.Claims not before the court.

Fair.Factual, public-interest, no guilt implied.
Killer driver smiles as charges are read
Prejudicial.“Killer” + demeanour = judgement.

Part 5

Your turn to report

Your notes · fictional case

The State v. K. Adeyemi

FICTIONAL · TRAINING ONLY

Court: High Court 3. Started 2 hours late — judge cited a power outage.

Charges: Conspiracy, false pretences, stealing 48 million from a market women’s cooperative. Pleaded not guilty.

Custody: Held since last November; bail refused in January.

Witness: Treasurer Mrs R. Eze, 56: “We gave him our contributions every Friday. In January, the account was empty.”

Cross-exam: She never saw the defendant withdraw money.

Exhibit: Bank statement, admitted.

Gallery: ~40 members; some wept. One shouted “Thief!” — warned by judge.

Outside: Defence counsel: “My client is a scapegoat.”

Next witness: A 15-year-old girl. Her mother gave you her name and school.

Adjourned to 9 December.
