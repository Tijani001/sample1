Uncovering Local Bias & Harm

Identify the tech & the mechanism

Research the AI technology in question and track how the automated system’s logic interprets human activities.

Explain context blind spots

Demystify the technical problem behind every bias through close observation and experts who know how the model was built — e.g. why systems trained outside Africa miss local human patterns, off-grid settings and cultural context.

Probe institutional denial

Document official silence and denial. What authorities refuse to say is part of the story.

HumAngle × Pulitzer Center · Drone Wars

03

Three takeaways. First, identify the technology and the mechanism: research the AI system and track how its automated logic interprets human activity. Second, explain the context blind spots: demystify the technical problem behind each bias by observing closely and speaking to experts who understand how the model was made. For instance, AI drone systems trained outside Africa can fail to recognise local human patterns, off-grid environments and cultural contexts. Third, probe institutional denial: document official silence and denial as evidence in itself.
