Find physical evidence of harm

Combine remote AI analysis with direct field reporting to verify physical evidence on the ground.

Step 2

Collaborative journalism

The method moves from ground truth to verification to collaboration. Step one: find physical evidence of harm by pairing remote AI analysis with field reporting. Step two: use accessible OSINT and GEOINT — free or low-cost satellite imagery, blast geometry, digital flight-tracking — to test official claims. Step three: don’t do it alone. Partner with local newsrooms, regional networks and global hubs like the Pulitzer Center to share data and safety resources.
