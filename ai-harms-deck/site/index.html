01 · Key takeaways

Uncovering Local Bias & Harm

Identify the tech & the mechanism

Research the AI technology in question and track how the automated system’s logic interprets human activities.

Explain context blind spots

Demystify the technical problem behind every bias through close observation and experts who know how the model was built — e.g. why systems trained outside Africa miss local human patterns, off-grid settings and cultural context.

Probe institutional denial

Document official silence and denial. What authorities refuse to say is part of the story.

HumAngle × Pulitzer Center · Drone Wars

03

Three takeaways. First, identify the technology and the mechanism: research the AI system and track how its automated logic interprets human activity. Second, explain the context blind spots: demystify the technical problem behind each bias by observing closely and speaking to experts who understand how the model was made. For instance, AI drone systems trained outside Africa can fail to recognise local human patterns, off-grid environments and cultural contexts. Third, probe institutional denial: document official silence and denial as evidence in itself.

03 · Key methodological steps

Conducting Investigations on AI Harms & Bias

Step 1

Find physical evidence of harm

Combine remote AI analysis with direct field reporting to verify physical evidence on the ground.

Step 2

Accessible OSINT & GEOINT

Use free or low-cost satellite imagery, blast geometry and digital flight-tracking to verify official claims.

Step 3

Collaborative journalism

Partner with local newsrooms, regional networks and global hubs such as the Pulitzer Center to share data and safety resources.

HumAngle × Pulitzer Center · Drone Wars

06

The method moves from ground truth to verification to collaboration. Step one: find physical evidence of harm by pairing remote AI analysis with field reporting. Step two: use accessible OSINT and GEOINT — free or low-cost satellite imagery, blast geometry, digital flight-tracking — to test official claims. Step three: don’t do it alone. Partner with local newsrooms, regional networks and global hubs like the Pulitzer Center to share data and safety resources.

04 · Practical steps

3 Key Techniques for Reporting on AI Systems

01

Audit the training data

Question where the algorithms were trained, and why they fail in African operational environments.

02

Follow the paper & money trail

Trace procurement contracts, bilateral trade agreements, hardware origin tags and tech-transfer deals.

03

Confront official narratives

Match government and military press releases line by line against verified field evidence and forensic testimonies.

HumAngle × Pulitzer Center · Drone Wars

07

Three techniques to take back to your newsroom. Audit the training data: where were these algorithms trained, and why do they fail here? Follow the paper and money trail: procurement contracts, bilateral trade agreements, hardware origin tags, tech-transfer deals. Confront official narratives: put every press release side by side with your verified field evidence and forensic testimony, line by line.
